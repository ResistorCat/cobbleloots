### Gameplay Changes

- **Valuable Items in Loot Balls**: Loot Balls can now contain valuable items, and rarer balls hold rarer treasures.
  - Common balls: Stardust and Pearl. Uncommon balls: Star Piece, Big Pearl and Nugget. Rare balls: Big Nugget, Rare Bone and Balm Mushroom. Master Loot Balls: Comet Shard and Pearl String.
  - Lure and Dive Loot Balls, which can be fished up with Poké Rods, hold sea treasures: Pearl, Big Pearl, Rare Bone and Pearl String.
  - The odds of medicine, candies, berries and EV items are unchanged; valuable items take part of the chance of getting the ball's own Poké Ball.
